Moonlit Echoes iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Moonlit Echoes for iOS is a paid, ad-free sleep sounds app. This policy explains how the iOS version handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The iOS version does not use advertising SDKs, does not display ads and does not collect advertising identifiers. The app stores user-selected preferences such as favorites, language and timer settings locally on the device.

How We Use Information

  • To provide local audio playback, favorites, language selection and sleep timer features.
  • To maintain app stability and improve the app experience.
  • To support user-selected settings on the device.

Advertising and Tracking

The iOS version does not display advertising, does not use Google AdMob, does not request tracking permission and does not personalize advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The iOS version does not use third-party advertising services. If Apple provides crash diagnostics or purchase processing information, that data is handled under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Moonlit Echoes is not specifically targeted at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
